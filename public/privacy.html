Flip Tiles: Privacy policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Flip Tiles is a puzzle game. There are no accounts and no analytics. The app shows an ad only when you choose to watch one for coins (below), and sends your game results only if you join the online leaderboards (below).

What stays on your device

Your progress (stars, coins, hints, the skins you own, your solutions, Codex entries and ranked games) is saved on your own device. Deleting the app deletes it.

The online leaderboards (only if you join)

Ranked mode can be played without them. If you choose to join the online leaderboards, the game sends the following to its server (hosted by Netlify):

  • the name you choose (shown publicly on the leaderboards; it doesn't have to be your real name);
  • a random number made by the game for your device, to keep your games together (it is not linked to your phone, your accounts or you, and it is never shown to anyone);
  • your ranked games: the puzzle, your moves, your time and when you played.

This is used only to show the leaderboards. It is not shared or sold. To have your name and games removed from the server, contact us (below) with your name on the leaderboards.

Ads (only when you choose to watch one)

In the app you can watch a short video ad to get coins. Ads are never shown unless you tap to watch one. The ads come from Google AdMob. When an ad is loaded, Google receives information from your device, such as its advertising ID, IP address and general device information, to show the ad, prevent fraud and measure it, as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before the first ad, and you can change your choice at any time under Privacy settings on the home screen. Without consent, ads are not personalised.
  • On iPhone, the app asks whether it may track you (App Tracking Transparency). If you say no, you still get ads and coins, but they are not personalised.
  • You can also reset or turn off your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

The game itself keeps only how many ads you watched (on your device), to give the right number of coins.

Purchases

Coins can be bought in the app. Payments are handled entirely by the App Store (Apple) or Google Play (Google), under their own privacy policies; the game never sees your payment details. The game only receives a purchase number from the store, which it keeps on your device so that each purchase is added once.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. The game itself collects no data from anyone who doesn't join the leaderboards; ads are only loaded when a player chooses to watch one.

Changes

If this changes, this page will be updated before that version of the game is released, and anything shared will be opt-in.
