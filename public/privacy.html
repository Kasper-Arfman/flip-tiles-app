Flip Tiles: Privacy policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Flip Tiles is a puzzle game. It does not collect, store elsewhere, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Your progress (stars, coins, hints, the skins you own, your solutions and Codex entries) is saved on your own device only. It never leaves it. Deleting the app deletes it.

Purchases

Coins can be bought in the app. Payments are handled entirely by the App Store (Apple) or Google Play (Google), under their own privacy policies; the game never sees your payment details. The game only receives a purchase number from the store, which it keeps on your device so that each purchase is added once.

Children

The game collects no data from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this ever changes (for example, an optional leaderboard), this page will be updated before that version of the game is released, and anything shared will be opt-in.
