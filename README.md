# Flip Tiles

**A puzzle of 25 tiles, two colors and one goal: make them all the same.**

Tap a tile and it flips, together with its four neighbors. Tap and hold to *force* a single tile.
Every board can be solved; the question is how few moves you need.

## The game

- **Hundreds of hand-picked levels** in twelve stages, Alpha to Mu, from a gentle start to boards
  that take real thought. Each level has three stars: solve it, solve it in the fewest moves, and
  solve it with flips alone.
- **Ranked mode**: random puzzles against the clock, with online leaderboards for every
  difficulty and a list of the best players overall.
- **The Codex**: draw any pattern on a solved board and the game shows you how to make it, a
  growing guide for the harder stages.
- **Your own look**: dozens of tile colors and shapes to collect, from hearts and paws to a
  strawberry.
- **Hints when you're stuck**, one move at a time, and a replay of your best solves.
- Plays offline, with sounds, on phones and in the browser.
