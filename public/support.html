Flip Tiles: Support

Questions, a problem, or an idea? Write to kaskiller3@gmail.com. Please say which phone or tablet you use, and the version number at the bottom of the home screen.

How do I play?

Tap a tile to flip it and its four neighbours. Make every tile the same color to solve the puzzle. In levels that allow it, tap and hold a tile to force it: that changes only that one tile. The ? button on the home screen shows it step by step.

I'm stuck on a level

Use a hint (it shows one move of a best solution), or open the Codex on the level to learn the patterns. A free video gives the coins for a hint.

I bought coins, but they didn't arrive

Close the app completely and open it again: a purchase that was interrupted is finished then. If they still don't show, write to us with the date of the purchase. Payments are handled by the App Store or Google Play; the receipt they emailed you helps.

Will I keep my progress on a new phone?

Your progress and coins are saved on your device only (there are no accounts), so deleting the app or changing phones starts over. Coins can't be restored by the store, because they are used up as you play.

Remove my name from the leaderboards

Write to us with your name on the leaderboards, and we remove your name and games. See also the privacy policy.
